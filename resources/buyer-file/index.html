ABOUT US

Welcome to Primeland Hotel Comfort in Every Stay

We are a small boutique-style hotel in the heart of Moshi Town, Kilimanjaro – Tanzania. Conveniently located approximately 1.5 km from Moshi's town center and just 45 minutes from Kilimanjaro International Airport. Whether you're here on a business trip, family visit, Kilimanjaro trek or safari, our well-trained team is ready to make your stay exceptional.

SERVICES

Our Services

At Primeland Hotel, every detail is thoughtfully curated from exquisite interior design to personalized service to ensure a delightful experience while travelling to Moshi.

Swimming Pool & Pool Bar

Restaurant & Daily Breakfast

Enjoy delicious meals from our on-site restaurant. Free daily breakfast is included with every room booking.
